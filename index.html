I · The Vision

For centuries, the tables of power have been set by the few and denied to the many. archonMUN was built to flip that table.

We gather young diplomats, agitators, and idealists who believe an order built on privilege and inherited power has run out of excuses. This October, delegates will stand for the nations too often spoken over: the Global South, the displaced, the dismissed.

We do not ask for a seat at the table. We rewrite it.

The archons were the nine magistrates of ancient Athens. They held the pen. They decided which cases were heard and which were quietly filed away.

We took the name so we could hand the pen to somebody else.

VI · The Programme

Three days,
gavel to gavel.

A provisional shape of the weekend. Exact times land in the delegate handbook once committee assignments are out.

  1. Day I Friday, 23 October
    • Registration and delegate briefing. Badges, handbooks, first coffee, first alliances.
    • Opening ceremony. Secretariat address and the keynote on rewriting the order.
    • Committee session I. Roll call, setting the agenda, opening speeches.
  2. Day II Saturday, 24 October
    • Committee sessions II and III. Moderated caucus, bloc formation, working papers.
    • Crisis escalations. Directives land without warning across the advanced rooms.
    • Delegate social. Off the record, out of the placard, still negotiating.
  3. Day III Sunday, 25 October
    • Committee sessions IV and V. Draft resolutions, amendments, final voting procedure.
    • Closing ceremony and awards. Best Delegate, Outstanding Delegate, Honourable Mention.
    • Farewell. The gavel falls. The arguments do not.
